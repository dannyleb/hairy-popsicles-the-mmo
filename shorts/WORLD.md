# Hairy Popsicles: the world (bible, draft 1)

The world of the game and the show, worked out: what it is, who lives in it,
where, what it sounds like, what's wrong with it, and how we make video from it.
Everything here grows out of what already exists (the MMO, the 11 episodes, the
cast, the art); the rest is new and up for review.

---

## 1. The idea in one paragraph

The world is the skin of something enormous that is asleep. Nobody has seen
all of it, nobody knows what it is, and nobody says its name. Everyone in it
**grew out of the ground**: someone plants seeds in the skin, they come up as
people, and one day they are **picked**. Nobody knows where the picked go.
The ground itches, swells, scabs over and opens up, and everyone treats this
as weather. Under the skin there is more: veins, fat, gut, bone, and things
that live down there. The residents are polite, small and anxious, and they
accept all of it. The horror is never the point of the scene. It's the floor
the scene stands on.

## 2. The rules of the world

The logic everything obeys. Each rule comes from the game; the show treats
it as fact and never explains it.

- **Everyone is grown.** You come up out of a plot in the skin, already the
  shape you'll always be. Nobody was a child. Nerm "hasn't slept since he was
  drawn"; the residents use *drawn* and *grown* to mean the same thing.
- **The Gardener.** Someone plants the seeds, tends the plots and harvests.
  Never seen, only felt: a shadow passing over Freckle Flats, the plot next to
  yours suddenly empty. (In the game, the Gardener is the player.)
- **Getting picked.** Harvested residents vanish. The polite word is
  *picked*. Some hope for it, most dread it. The rumour is that the picked are
  pressed flat in **the Album**: a book where you stay, mid-expression,
  forever. (The game's album sets are that book.)
- **The ground is alive and acts up.** Boils swell and must be lanced. Scabs
  crust and must be peeled. Gashes open with crumbs in them. Ticks burrow,
  and if the head stays in, they come back. Hairs grow inward. Blackheads.
  Navel lint drifts like tumbleweed. Residents deal with it the way people deal
  with roadworks.
- **Weather is the body.** Dandruff falls like snow. A sweaty fog rolls in.
  An itchy wind. **Pus Rain.** And the **Blood Moon**, when everything goes
  red and quiet and the Eye opens.
- **The Itch.** Now and then the whole world itches. Everyone has to work
  together to calm it (plant, harvest, tend). If they fail, **the Scratch**
  comes: nails the size of mountains, raking the surface. Nobody alive has
  seen a Scratch. The Aunties have.
- **The Eye.** At the Navel, an eyeball is set in the ground, mostly shut.
  It's the only part of the sleeper that looks back. When it opens, everyone
  stops talking.
- **Under the Skin.** Going *down* is a real thing and slightly shameful,
  like a back alley. The ones who come back are different. "I've never been
  this deep before" (Brayden, Ep4) is said the way you'd say it about a
  basement club.
- **Nobody sleeps.** There's no night as we know it, only the hours when the
  plots are quiet. Counting sheep is something people try. It goes badly.

## 3. Tone (the rules of the show)

- **Dry, polite, wrong.** Characters accept the unacceptable. The joke is the
  understatement; the dread is what nobody mentions.
- **Never explain.** No lore dumps. One wrong detail per shot is better than
  five.
- **Close and wet.** ASMR intimacy: every mouth sound, every drip. The camera is
  too close to faces. The room is always slightly sticky.
- **Calm before frantic.** Long, still, uncomfortable beats, then one sudden
  violent thing, then back to calm as if nothing happened.
- **VHS.** Everything feels found: tracking lines, static, tape that shouldn't
  exist.
- **Influences** (tone only, never copied): David Firth, Don Hertzfeldt, David
  Lynch, Eraserhead, *Tim and Eric* public-access dread, old BBC public
  information films, nature documentaries narrated far too calmly.

## 4. The residents

Each entry: who they are, what they want, what's wrong, their tic, and where
they are now in the story so far.

### The original cast

**Gub** (toad): the first one, the star of the original game ("Feed Gub").
- **Who:** fat, smug, croaking, always hungry. The mouth of the world.
- **Wants:** more. Of anything.
- **Wrong:** whatever he eats keeps talking inside him.
- **Tic:** burps between words; refers to his belly in the plural.
- **Now:** Glorp lives in his stomach (Ep6) and offers commentary.

**Glorp** (the roast)
- **Who:** a lumpy pink roasted blob in a puddle of his own juices. Kind,
  serene, gurgling. No mouth; the voice comes up through the fat.
- **Wants:** to be useful. He believes being eaten is what he's for.
- **Wrong:** he's back on the platter every Sunday, whatever happened the
  week before. Nobody asks how.
- **Tic:** says "Afternoon." whatever the time.
- **Now:** inside Gub, and also on Nana's table next Sunday.

**Spoon Nana**
- **Who:** the matriarch. Floral dress, forty-a-day wheeze, a spoon for every
  occasion.
- **Wants:** everyone fed, everyone at her table, nobody leaving.
- **Wrong:** her cooking talks. The rumour (never confirmed) is that the
  picked end up in her kitchen.
- **Tic:** "You ate the soup." Calls the sleeper *Mother*.
- **Now:** Sunday roast is every Sunday.

### The anxious

**Dennis** (sweats)
- **Who:** clammy, blue-sweating, desperate to be employed.
- **Wants:** a job, any job, to be *placed*.
- **Wrong:** leaves a damp mark on everything, and he's terrified it's
  permanent.
- **Tic:** apologises in triplicate.
- **Now:** still waiting to hear back from the interview (Ep2).

**Orville** (droopy)
- **Who:** a long sagging face, heavy lids. A functionary at **the Office**
  in the Armpit, which decides who is planted where.
- **Wants:** to finish one sentence before falling asleep. He never has.
- **Wrong:** the sag is getting lower each episode. Something is pulling him
  down.
- **Tic:** "Where do you see yourself in five..."
- **Now:** still mid-interview with Dennis.

**Pud** (weeper)
- **Who:** a lopsided swollen head, weeping blue tears. Fragile, sincere.
- **Wants:** someone to tell him he looks normal.
- **Wrong:** he asked the mirror once. Now his face is "a bit better."
- **Tic:** "Do my tears look normal to you?"
- **Now:** his eye has moved (Ep8). He's started to like it.

**Teddy** (screamer)
- **Who:** a skull-like face locked in a scream. The alarm.
- **Wants:** to be calm. He is calm, inside.
- **Wrong:** he screams at everything harmless and is perfectly calm at real
  horror. Learn to read Teddy backwards.
- **Tic:** "AAAH. Sorry. That's just my resting face."
- **Now:** standing at the end of Nerm's bed? (Ep5: was the fourth sheep him?)

**Brayden** (pimples)
- **Who:** a young man, all pimples, voice cracking. Deeply in love with
  Duane.
- **Wants:** to be worthy of her.
- **Wrong:** everything he feels comes out through his skin, literally.
- **Tic:** "Don't pick. Don't pick. Don't pick."
- **Now:** dating Duane (Ep4). The pimple was a gift. She kept it.

**Duane** (melter)
- **Who:** she's melting, one eye, sliding lower every scene. Sad and tender.
- **Wants:** to be seen as she is right now, before she changes again.
- **Wrong:** she's slowly becoming a puddle, and she knows roughly when.
- **Tic:** "Lower than when you picked me up."
- **Now:** with Brayden, "a candle with a face." They go down to the Deep on
  dates.

### The strange

**Greg** (bigeye)
- **Who:** one enormous bulging eye, gritted yellow teeth, gravel voice.
- **Wants:** to close it. He can't; there's no lid.
- **Wrong:** he sees things nobody else can: the Gardener's shadow, the
  fourth sheep, the Hand. Nobody believes him because he's rude.
- **Tic:** "What are YOU looking at?"
- **Now:** owns scab sunglasses (Ep1) he refuses to wear, and wears them at
  night.

**Doolie** (conehead)
- **Who:** cone head, manic grin, sing-song, spit on every S. Makes presents
  out of himself.
- **Wants:** to be loved back.
- **Wrong:** the grin never drops, not even when he cries. "Happy tears.
  Probably."
- **Tic:** "I made you something."
- **Now:** working on a new present for Greg. It's bigger.

**Nerm** (redeye)
- **Who:** huge yellow eyes, a cut across his face, whispering. Hasn't slept
  since he was drawn.
- **Wants:** one night's sleep.
- **Wrong:** he is the only one awake in the quiet hours, so he's the only
  one who knows what walks the Skin then.
- **Tic:** "...same time tomorrow, then."
- **Now:** visited every night by the fourth sheep (Ep5).

**Pim** (mirror)
- **Who:** a pale lumpy face with eyes in the wrong places. Lives in
  reflective surfaces. Too calm, too close.
- **Wants:** everyone to match.
- **Wrong:** whatever looks at Pim long enough starts to rearrange.
- **Tic:** repeats you, slightly wrong. "I only show what you bring."
- **Now:** **in every reflection in the show.** A puddle, a wine glass, a
  spoon. Hidden cameos from here on.

**Marla** (tendrils)
- **Who:** a pink fleshy head trailing long hair-tendrils. Her voice comes
  from several places at once.
- **Wants:** to know what's living in her hair.
- **Wrong:** things grow in there. Family, apparently.
- **Tic:** "Pluck it. No, don't."
- **Now:** her brother has been fished out (Ep11). There are more.

**Mabel** (fairy)
- **Who:** a tiny gross fairy with an anglerfish lure on her antenna, sitting
  on a mossy mushroom. Speaks in a grown man's gravelly put-on sweet voice.
  Nobody comments on it.
- **Wants:** a catch.
- **Wrong:** she fishes in people.
- **Tic:** "Make a wish. Make it gross."
- **Now:** caught Marla's brother. Wants the big one.

**Fergal** (leprechaun)
- **Who:** ginger beard, green cap, cackling. Grants wishes; collects gold
  teeth.
- **Wants:** your teeth.
- **Wrong:** every wish comes true, exactly, cruelly.
- **Tic:** "Ah, now that one's free."
- **Now:** his pot of gold teeth is fuller than last time.

**Drak** (stubble)
- **Who:** a bald, exhausted man's head, heavy stubble.
- **Wants:** a shave and a sit-down.
- **Wrong:** his stubble is growing inwards (Ep7). He can feel it.
- **Tic:** "...still itchy."
- **Now:** itchy. It's spreading.

**Shiv**
- **Who:** skinny, wrinkled, a long fleshy nose that grows with every lie,
  always talking with his mouth full.
- **Wants:** to be liked. He lies to get there.
- **Wrong:** the nose goes *somewhere* when it grows. Last time it went into
  Hork's brain.
- **Tic:** "It's nice."
- **Now:** living inside Hork's brain (Ep3). "It's nice in here."

### The family (the Wart Hills aristocracy)

**Hork** (snoot), **Uncle Hork**, **Cousin Hork**
- **Who:** exposed brains, trunk-noses, congested snobbery. Old money in the
  Wart Hills. Brain folds are status.
- **Wants:** more folds than the others.
- **Wrong:** Hork has a lodger in his brain (Shiv), the family know, and
  nobody says so at dinner.
- **Tic:** wet sniffs; "the architecture."
- **Now:** Cousin's brain is "minimalist." Uncle has forty-one folds.

**The Aunties** (head-bloom)
- **Who:** a cluster of tiny skulls blooming on a spiny stalk, with moth
  wings. A chorus: one dry voice and many tiny ones in unison.
- **Wants:** nothing. They already know how it ends.
- **Wrong:** they remember the last Scratch.
- **Tic:** "We knew. We always know."
- **Use:** the show's narrators and omens. They can close any episode.

### The outsiders

**Zib** (antenna)
- **Who:** a skinny red alien, one crackling antenna, a loincloth.
  Crash-landed, thinks the Skin is Earth.
- **Wants:** to report back.
- **Wrong:** "Earth is easy." He's wrong, and his reports get stranger.
- **Use:** an anthropologist's voice for documentary formats.

**Doug** (bricks)
- **Who:** a square head of cracked fleshy bricks, a toothy grin, and every
  word a separate block.
- **Wants:** to stay standing.
- **Wrong:** collapsed (Ep10). He rebuilds himself, but one brick is always
  missing.
- **Tic:** "There's a crack. Don't. Mention. The crack."

**Skitch** (bat-rat)
- **Who:** bat-eared, rat-faced, big orange eyes. Squeaks, scavenges, moves
  into holes.
- **Wants:** a hole of his own.
- **Wrong:** he finds holes in people.
- **Now:** lives in the hole where Doug's head was.

## 6. The sound of it

- **Voices:** every character has a designed voice (`shorts/voices.json`), an
  ASMR version, and their own effects. Mabel's is a man's.
- **Mouth:** ASMR closeness; saliva; chewing (Shiv); the saliva bed.
- **Rooms:** each set has its own looped room tone (`shorts/sounds/`).
- **Music:** the game's Suno tracks, slowed and detuned (creepy-box,
  creepy-theme); only under titles.
- **Signature sounds to make next:**
  - **The heartbeat:** under everything in the Deep, slow.
  - **The Itch:** a rising dry scratching from all directions.
  - **The Boil:** a stretching creak.
  - **The Eye:** a wet click when it opens.
  - **The Scratch:** a sound too big to hear. Felt as a pressure drop, then
    silence.
- **VHS:** static bursts, hiss and tracking noise between scenes.

## 7. Video with Higgsfield: how to use it to its fullest

Our kit makes **dialogue**: characters on a green screen, lip-synced, over a
still painted room. That's why the episodes are small. Higgsfield's strength
is the opposite: **cinematic motion**, camera moves, scale and macro
photoreal texture. So we split the work:

- **Higgsfield** makes the picture the kit can't: sweeping establishing shots
  of the Skin, crash zooms and dolly moves, crowds, weather, the Boil
  erupting, the Eye opening, the Scratch, characters moving through real 3D
  space.
- **Our kit** keeps doing what it's good at: designed voices, lip-sync for
  dialogue, ASMR mix, captions, VHS, end cards.

(What exactly the Higgsfield connector exposes, which models, presets and
references, gets confirmed at the start of the Higgsfield session.)

### Formats that suit it

1. **The Itch** (trailer, 60–90 s, 16:9 and 9:16): the season's teaser. The
   world explained without explaining.
2. **Public information films:** "What to Do in an Itch", "Your Boil and
   You", "Getting Picked: A Guide for the Family". Calm narrator (the
   Aunties), cheerful 1970s graphics, horrifying footage.
3. **Skin News at Six:** a local newsreader (Orville, asleep by the end),
   weather (dandruff, Pus Rain warnings), reports from Freckle Flats.
4. **Adverts:** Spoon Nana's Sunday Soup ("You ate the soup."), Fergal's
   Wishes ("Terms apply. Teeth required."), the Office ("Get Placed").
5. **Nature documentary:** *The Fat Fields*, narrated by Zib: wildlife of the
   Deep, mites grazing, a tick migration.
6. **Found tapes:** "TAPE 7 — THE KNOT." Handheld descent Under the Skin,
   someone breathing, the light failing.
7. **Nerm's night cam:** fixed security-camera footage of the quiet hours, 3
   a.m. timestamps, something crossing the plots.

### Recurring motifs to plant in everything

- **Pim** in a reflection (a puddle, a spoon, a wine glass, a window).
- **The fourth sheep** standing very still somewhere in the background.
- **The Gardener's shadow** passing over the ground.
- **An empty plot** where someone used to be.
- **The Eye**, slightly more open in every film.

### A plan for the three trial days

- **Day 1: references and tests.** Upload the character renders and sets;
  test which Higgsfield modes keep characters on-model; shots 1, 2 and 7 of
  the teaser as tests.
- **Day 2: the teaser.** Shoot the rest of THE ITCH; one public information
  film ("What to Do in an Itch").
- **Day 3: finish and publish.** Voices and the mix in our kit, captions, VHS,
  end card; the teaser and PSA on hairypopsicles.com/episodes.

Spend the trial on the shots only Higgsfield can make. The dialogue stays in
our kit, where it's cheap.
