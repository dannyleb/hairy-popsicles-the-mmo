title: Liar
background: surface

SNOOT (snooty, congested): Shiv. You've noticed.

SNOOT (snooty, slow): The ring, Shiv. In the nose. It came off a bull.
SHIV (too smooth): Oh. Yeah. It's... nice.

SNOOT (snooty): Say it slower.
SHIV (strained, close): It's the most beautiful thing I've ever—
> SNOOT & SHIV (3s, shake): Shiv's long nose shoots across the gap and jabs straight into Snoot's exposed brain
> SNOOT (1.5s, still, close)
SNOOT (calmly, congested): ...Shiv. You're in my brain.
