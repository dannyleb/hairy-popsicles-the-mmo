# Hairy Popsicles, episode 3. Shiv's nose grows when he lies. Hork's brain is right there.
title: Liar
scene: A pretentious velvet parlour: deep crimson walls of wrinkled skin, a chandelier made of teeth, gilded frames holding oil paintings of noses, a fireplace oozing something thick, dim candlelight
sounds: a stuffy old parlour, a fireplace crackling with something thick bubbling and oozing in it, a slow grandfather clock ticking, a wet drip falling from the chandelier
room: large

HORK (snooty, congested): Shiv. You've noticed.
SHIV (sly): Noticed what.
HORK (snooty, slow): The ring, Shiv. In the nose. It came off a bull.
SHIV (too smooth): Oh. Yeah. It's... nice.
> SHIV (2s): his long fleshy nose twitches and grows a little longer
HORK (snooty, push): Most faces can't carry it. You need the... architecture.
SHIV (strained): Mm-hm. Really. Suits you.
> SHIV (2s, push): his nose stretches out even longer, quivering and wet
HORK (snooty): Say it slower.
SHIV (strained, close): It's the most beautiful thing I've ever—
> HORK & SHIV (3s, shake): Shiv's long nose shoots across the gap and jabs straight into Hork's exposed brain
> HORK (1.5s, still, close)
HORK (calmly, congested): ...Shiv. You're in my brain.
SHIV (softly, sly): I know. It's nice in here.
> SHIV (2s): his nose grows even longer
