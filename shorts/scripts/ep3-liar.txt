# Hairy Popsicles, episode 3. Shiv's nose grows when he lies. Snoot's brain is right there.
title: Liar
background: surface

SNOOT (snooty, congested): Shiv. Darling. Do you like my new nose ring?
SHIV (sly): I love it.
> SHIV (2s): his long fleshy nose twitches and grows a little longer
SNOOT (snooty): You hate it.
SHIV (too smooth): No. It's beautiful.
> SHIV (2s, push): his nose stretches out even longer, quivering
SNOOT (offended): Shiv.
SHIV (strained, close): It's the best... nose ring... I have ever...
> SNOOT & SHIV (3s, shake): Shiv's long nose shoots out across the gap and jabs into Snoot's exposed brain
SNOOT (screaming, punch, shake): MY BRAIN! YOU STABBED ME IN THE BRAIN!
SHIV (sly, close): ...I'm fine.
> SHIV (2s): his nose grows even longer
