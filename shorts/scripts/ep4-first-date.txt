# Hairy Popsicles, episode 4. Pimples and Melter, a first date, under the skin.
title: First Date
background: deep

PIMPLES (nervous, voice cracking): You look... really nice tonight, Melter.
MELTER (melting, sad): Thank you. I did my face this morning. Most of it's still here.
> MELTER (3s): her face slowly slides downward, one eye drooping lower and lower
PIMPLES (nervous): Is it warm in here, or...
MELTER (flat): It's me. I'm the warm.
PIMPLES (voice cracking, push): I have to tell you something. I'm... ripe.
MELTER (horrified, whispering, close): ...don't.
PIMPLES (screaming, punch, shake): IT'S HAPPENING!
> PIMPLES (2s, shake): a big pimple on his cheek bursts with a wet splatter
> MELTER (2s, still, close, push)
MELTER (moved, tearful): ...that's the nicest thing anyone's ever done for me.
