# Hairy Popsicles, episode 4. Pimples and Melter, a first date, under the skin.
title: First Date
scene: A romantic restaurant deep inside a body: red fleshy cavern walls threaded with pulsing veins, a small table with a white tablecloth stained red, a single candle and two wine glasses of dark red liquid, warm intimate candlelight
ambience: gut

PIMPLES (nervous, voice cracking): I've, um... never been this deep before.
MELTER (melting, sad): It's quieter down here. Nobody looks at you.
> PIMPLES (1.5s, still, push)
PIMPLES (nervous): You look really... your face is very...
MELTER (flat): Lower than when you picked me up. I know.
> MELTER (3s): her face slowly slides downward, one eye drooping lower and lower
PIMPLES (nervous, push): No— it's good! It's like a candle. A candle with a face.
> MELTER (2s, still, close)
MELTER (softly, moved): ...nobody's ever called me a candle.
PIMPLES (voice cracking, close): Melter. I've been holding something in all night—
MELTER (whispering): Don't.
PIMPLES (screaming, punch, shake): I CAN'T HOLD IT—
> PIMPLES (2s, shake): a big pimple on his cheek bursts with a wet splatter
> MELTER (2s, still, close, push)
MELTER (softly, tearful): ...is that for me?
