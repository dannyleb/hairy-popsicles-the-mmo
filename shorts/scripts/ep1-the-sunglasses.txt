# Hairy Popsicles, episode 1. Big Eye's eye won't close. Conehead tries to help.
title: The Sunglasses
background: surface

> BIG EYE & CONEHEAD (3s): Conehead bounces up to Big Eye, grinning, hiding something behind his back
CONEHEAD (manic, too cheerful): Big Eye! Big Eye! I got you a present!
BIG EYE (suspicious): ...what is it.
CONEHEAD (giggling): Sunglasses! For your eye!
> BIG EYE (1.5s, still, push)
BIG EYE (flat): Which eye.
> CONEHEAD (1.2s, still, close)
CONEHEAD (whispering, giggling): ...the small one.
BIG EYE (screaming, punch, shake): THE BIG ONE IS THE ONE THAT HURTS, CONEHEAD!
CONEHEAD (sing-song): Then close it!
BIG EYE (quiet, defeated): It doesn't close. It hasn't closed since nineteen ninety-four.
> BIG EYE (2s, still, close, push)
CONEHEAD (whispering, close): ...I'll get you a hat.
