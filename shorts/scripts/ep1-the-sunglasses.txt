# Hairy Popsicles, episode 1. Doolie has made Greg a present. Greg doesn't want it.
title: The Sunglasses
prop: scab sunglasses = a crude, lumpy little pair of sunglasses made of dried, crusty brown and dark red scabs pressed together, with only one small dark lens; the other side is just an empty crusty ring with no lens
scene: A blazing hot afternoon on a vast plain of sunburnt, peeling skin stretching to the horizon, a harsh white sun glaring in a sickly yellow sky, heat shimmer, a few long hairs standing up like dead trees, blisters and moles dotting the ground like boulders

> GREG (2s, still, push)
DOOLIE (off, softly): Greg... I made you something.
GREG (flat): No.
> GREG & DOOLIE (3s, with: scab sunglasses): Doolie slowly leans in much too close to Greg, grinning, the little scab sunglasses sitting on the ground between them
DOOLIE (softly, pleased): Sunglasses. For your eye.
> SCAB SUNGLASSES (3s, push): the crusty scab sunglasses glisten wetly in the heat, a bead of ooze slowly dripping off the frame
DOOLIE (off, softly, pleased): I made them out of my old scabs. Took me all summer.
GREG (flat): ...there's one lens.
DOOLIE (gently): Well... you've only got the one... nice... side.
GREG (low, dangerous, push): Say "nice side" again.
DOOLIE (whispering, delighted): Nice side—
GREG (screaming, punch, shake): IT HASN'T CLOSED SINCE NINETEEN NINETY-FOUR!
> DOOLIE (1.5s, still, push)
DOOLIE (softly, close): ...put them on, Greg.
> GREG (2s, still, close, push, wearing: scab sunglasses)
GREG (quietly, surprised, wearing: scab sunglasses): ...they're warm.
DOOLIE (off, whispering): That's me.
