# Hairy Popsicles, episode 5. Red Eye hasn't slept since he was drawn.
title: Four Sheep
background: black
facing: front
ambience: hum

> RED EYE (2s, still, push)
RED EYE (whispering, unblinking): Haven't slept. Not since they drew me.
RED EYE (flat): Doctor says count sheep. So... I count them.
> RED EYE (1.5s, still, push)
RED EYE (whispering): One sheep. Two sheep. Three sheep has my mum's face. That's fine. That's fine.
RED EYE (whispering, close): Four sheep doesn't jump the fence.
RED EYE (whispering, close, push): Four sheep just stands at the end of the bed. Breathing.
> RED EYE (2s, still, close, push)
RED EYE (politely, barely audible): ...hello, four sheep.
SCREAMER (screaming, close, punch, shake): AAAAAAAAAAAAAAH!
> RED EYE (1.5s, still, close)
RED EYE (flat): ...same time tomorrow, then.
