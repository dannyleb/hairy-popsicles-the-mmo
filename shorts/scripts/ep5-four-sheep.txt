# Hairy Popsicles, episode 5. Nerm hasn't slept since he was drawn.
title: Four Sheep
scene: A dark bedroom at three in the morning, seen from the pillow: a lumpy bed with stained sheets, wallpaper of peeling skin, a thin strip of blue moonlight through the curtains, and at the far end of the bed the faint dark silhouette of a sheep standing perfectly still
sounds: a silent bedroom at three in the morning, a fridge humming faintly through the wall, a clock ticking, and very close, the slow heavy breathing of a large animal in the dark
room: small
facing: front

> NERM (2s, still, push)
NERM (whispering, unblinking): Haven't slept. Not since they drew me.
NERM (flat): Doctor says count sheep. So... I count them.
> NERM (1.5s, still, push)
NERM (whispering, twitching, a manic little giggle): One sheep. Two sheep. Three sheep has my mum's face. That's fine. That's fine.
NERM (whispering, close): Four sheep doesn't jump the fence.
NERM (whispering, close, push): Four sheep just stands at the end of the bed. Breathing.
> NERM (2s, still, close, push)
NERM (politely, barely audible): ...hello, four sheep.
TEDDY (screaming, close, punch, shake): AAAAAAAAAAAAAAH!
> NERM (1.5s, still, close)
NERM (flat): ...same time tomorrow, then.
